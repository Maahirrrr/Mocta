FAQ

QUESTIONS,
answered.

Have an unconventional project or unique requirement? Reach out directly or write to hello@mocta.design.

How does Mocta differ from conventional web agencies?

Most agencies either operate as pure graphic designers without engineering depth or as offshore dev teams using cookie-cutter templates. Mocta combines fine-arts aesthetic discipline with engineering in AI and behavioral psychology. You get timeless design and systems that actually automate your back office.

How long does a typical project take?

Focused landing pages and brand foundations take two weeks. Comprehensive multi-page platforms with custom integrations and AI workflows take three to five weeks from scope approval to production launch.

What kinds of AI automations do you build?

We build pragmatic back-office automations: instant proposal and estimate generators for customer inquiries, document summarizers, automated CRM routing, and customized LLM assistants that draft replies for your team to review and approve.

Who will be working on our project?

You work directly with the two studio principals. We do not pass your requirements down to junior subcontractors or account managers. Every layout, line of code, and automation model is reviewed and handled by the founders.

Do you assist with copywriting and messaging?

Yes. Informed by our background in cognitive and consumer psychology, we craft value propositions, headline hierarchies, and clear call-to-action microcopy that guide visitors toward conversion without relying on cheap marketing gimmicks.

What happens after launch?

We provide comprehensive documentation, handover walkthroughs, and optional monthly studio care covering hosting, security updates, continuous design iterations, and automation monitoring.
