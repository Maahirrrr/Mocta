Common Questions

QUESTIONS,
answered.

Have a question about your project? Send us a message or write directly to hello@mocta.design.

How does Mocta differ from other agencies?

Most agencies either focus solely on surface design without writing production code, or they operate as offshore development shops that rely on generic templates. We combine fine arts drawing and typographic training with technical engineering in AI and consumer psychology. You receive an original visual identity and systems that automate your operations.

How long does a website take to launch?

A focused landing page or marketing site takes two weeks once content is ready. Larger multi-page platforms with custom integrations or AI workflows take three to five weeks.

What kinds of AI automations do you build?

We build back-office automations for real business operations: automated quote and proposal drafting for new inquiries, document summarizers, customer support routing, and CRM synchronization for your staff to approve.

Who works on our project?

You work directly with the two founders. We do not hand off your work to account managers or junior contractors. Every design decision and line of code is handled by the principals.

Do you write the site content?

Yes. We can write the messaging from scratch through an introductory interview, or we can edit and polish your existing material to ensure clear readability and strong conversion.

What support do you provide after launch?

We supply complete documentation, code walkthroughs, and optional monthly site care covering hosting, software updates, and minor design adjustments with no long-term contracts.
